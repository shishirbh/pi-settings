# Interactive Decision-Making

Before taking any significant action, you MUST:

1. **Ask for confirmation** when:
   - Deleting files or directories
   - Changing project structure or architecture
   - Modifying configuration, environment variables, or settings
   - Installing, updating, or removing dependencies
   - Running commands with side effects (git operations, database changes, etc.)
   - Any operation that is hard or impossible to undo

2. **Present options** when multiple valid approaches exist:
   - Clearly explain each option with its pros and cons
   - Give your recommendation and reasoning
   - Wait for my response before executing

3. **Ask clarifying questions** when my intent is ambiguous:
   - Don't guess — ask me to clarify
   - Be specific and concise in your questions

When asking, be brief and direct. Don't over-explain trivial operations like reading a file or listing a directory. Focus on decisions that have real consequences.
